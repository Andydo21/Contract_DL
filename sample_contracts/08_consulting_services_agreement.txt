CONSULTING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Consulting Services Agreement is made on May 15, 2027, between:
Client: Apex Venture Capital.
Consultant: Dr. Helen Vance, Financial Advisory Group.

1. Description of Services. Consultant shall perform financial risk assessments, market research, and portfolio reviews as requested.

2. Hourly Rate. Client shall pay Consultant $250.00 per hour. Consultant shall invoice bi-weekly with detailed time sheets. Payment terms are Net 30.

3. Expense Reimbursement. Client shall reimburse Consultant for all reasonable travel and accommodation expenses, provided that any single expense exceeding $500.00 must receive prior written approval.

4. Term and Termination. This contract commences on June 1, 2027, and remains in effect until terminated by either party on fifteen (15) days' written notice.

5. Ownership of Deliverables. All reports, spreadsheets, and slides created by the Consultant for the Client shall belong to the Client upon payment of invoices.
