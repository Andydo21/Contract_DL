NON-DISCLOSURE AND CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of October 12, 2026, by and between:
Disclosing Party: Alpha Corp, having its principal place of business at 123 Tech Lane.
Receiving Party: Beta Solutions, having its principal place of business at 456 Partner Road.

1. Purpose. The Disclosing Party wishes to share certain proprietary and confidential information with the Receiving Party solely for evaluating a potential partnership.

2. Confidential Information. Confidential Information includes all technical data, trade secrets, software prototypes, business plans, and financial reports disclosed.

3. Obligations. The Receiving Party agrees:
a) To maintain strict confidentiality.
b) Not to use the information for its own commercial benefit.
c) To limit access to employees who need to know.

4. Penalties for Breach. In the event of a breach of confidentiality, the Receiving Party shall immediately pay the Disclosing Party a liquidated damages sum of $100,000.00. The Disclosing Party is also entitled to seek immediate injunctive relief without posting a bond.

5. Term. This Agreement and the confidentiality obligations shall remain in effect for ten (10) years from the date of disclosure.

Signed by authorized representatives of both parties.
