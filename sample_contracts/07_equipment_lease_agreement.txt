INDUSTRIAL EQUIPMENT LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is made on April 12, 2027, between:
Lessor: HeavyRent Logistics.
Lessee: BuildFast Construction.

1. Leased Equipment. Lessor leases to Lessee one (1) Caterpillar 320 Excavator.

2. Lease Term. Monthly rental, starting May 1, 2027, for a duration of six (6) months.

3. Rent. Lessee shall pay $5,500.00 per month in advance. Late payments shall incur a penalty of $100.00 per day.

4. Maintenance. Lessee is responsible for all operating costs, fuel, and daily maintenance. Any mechanical breakdowns must be repaired by Lessee at their own expense using authorized parts.

5. Liability. Lessee assumes all risks of loss or damage to the equipment and agrees to indemnify Lessor against any claims arising from its operation.
