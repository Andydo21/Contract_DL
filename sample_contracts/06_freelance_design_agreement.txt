FREELANCE GRAPHIC DESIGN CONTRACT

This Agreement is made on March 5, 2027, between:
Client: Spark Beverages.
Designer: Chloe Vance (Freelancer).

1. Services. Designer shall deliver brand identity designs, including logo, color palette, and package mockups.

2. Revisions. The project fee includes up to three (3) rounds of revisions. Any additional revisions will be billed at an hourly rate of $75.00.

3. Project Fee and Payment. The total project fee is $3,200.00.
- 50% deposit before design work begins.
- 50% balance payment due upon delivery of final source files.

4. Rights Transfer. Upon receipt of final payment in full, the Designer transfers all copyrights of the selected design to the Client. All preliminary concepts remain the property of the Designer.

5. Termination Fee. If the Client terminates this contract prior to completion, the Client must pay a kill fee of $1,000.00 in addition to the non-refundable deposit.
