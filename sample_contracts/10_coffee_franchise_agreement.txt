FRANCHISE AGREEMENT

This Franchise Agreement is entered into on July 10, 2027, by and between:
Franchisor: BrewHaven Cafe Franchise Corp.
Franchisee: Nguyen Van A.

1. Grant of Franchise. Franchisor grants Franchisee the right to operate one (1) BrewHaven Cafe outlet at 12 Nguyen Hue, District 1, HCMC.

2. Franchise Fees. Franchisee shall pay:
a) Initial Franchise Fee of $25,000.00.
b) Monthly Royalty Fee equal to 6% of gross sales.
c) Monthly Marketing Contribution equal to 2% of gross sales.

3. Proprietary Standards. Franchisee must purchase all coffee beans, cups, branding materials, and equipment exclusively from the Franchisor's approved distributors.

4. Term. The term of the franchise is five (5) years, renewable under renegotiated terms.

5. Termination. Franchisor may immediately terminate this agreement if the Franchisee fails to maintain quality standards or falls behind on royalty payments for more than 30 days.
