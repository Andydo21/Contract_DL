COMMERCIAL OFFICE LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is entered into on December 15, 2026, by and between:
Landlord: Apex Properties LLC.
Tenant: GreenTech Innovators Inc.

1. Premises. Landlord leases to Tenant Office Suite 402, located at 88 Tower Blvd, for office use.

2. Lease Term. The lease term shall be for three (3) years, commencing January 1, 2027, and terminating December 31, 2029.

3. Base Rent. Tenant shall pay Landlord a monthly base rent of $4,500.00, due on the first day of each month. 
Automatic Rent Increase: The monthly rent shall increase by 10% automatically on each anniversary of the commencement date.

4. Security Deposit. Tenant shall deposit $9,000.00 with Landlord as security for performance.

5. Maintenance and Repairs. Tenant is solely responsible for all maintenance, repairs, and utilities within the leased premises, including HVAC repairs and electrical maintenance.
