SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT

This Software Development Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on November 1, 2026, by and between:
Client: Delta Retail Ltd.
Developer: DevCraft Systems.

1. Scope of Work. Developer agrees to design, develop, and implement the e-commerce web platform as detailed in Exhibit A (Specifications).

2. Term and Delivery. The project must be completed and delivered on or before April 1, 2027. If delivery is delayed past this date, Developer shall pay Client $1,500.00 for each day of delay as liquidated damages.

3. Payment. Client shall pay Developer a total fee of $45,000.00, structured as follows:
- 20% Initial Deposit upon signing.
- 40% Upon successful completion of Beta release.
- 40% Upon final acceptance testing.

4. Intellectual Property. All code, designs, and documentation created under this Agreement shall belong exclusively to the Client only after final payment has been made in full.

5. Limitation of Liability. The maximum aggregate liability of the Developer under this Agreement for any claim whatsoever shall be capped at $500.00.
